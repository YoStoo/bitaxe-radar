Questions

What is a Bitaxe?

A small, open-source Bitcoin miner about the size of a phone. It uses about as much power as a light bulb and runs quietly on a desk. NerdQaxe and Nerdaxe are similar boards from other makers.

Can a small miner make money?

Not reliably. It "solo mines": if it ever finds a block it wins the whole reward (about 3.125 BTC), but the odds are tiny and electricity costs money every year. Think of it as a lottery ticket and a fun gadget, not income. Nothing here is financial advice.
