Your first hour with a Bitaxe

Plug it in and the screen shows a setup Wi-Fi name and a QR code. Join it from your phone and put the miner on your home Wi-Fi. It only works with 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.

Then open the miner's dashboard and change the pool's User field to your own Bitcoin address. Do the backup pool too. This is the step people skip, and it's where a block reward would go.

After about 10 minutes, check that accepted shares are going up and the hashrate looks normal for your board.
