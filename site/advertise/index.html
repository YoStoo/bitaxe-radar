Advertise on Bitaxe Radar

Bitaxe Radar tracks prices and stock for Bitaxe-class solo miners across 4 sellers and 20 listings. People land here when they are deciding where to buy, which is the moment a seller wants to be seen.

What is available

  • Featured seller spot on our 6 model pages, right under the price table, labelled "Sponsored".
  • Accessory spot for stands, cases, heatsinks, fans and power supplies.
  • Get listed in the tracker: if you sell Bitaxe-class miners and we don't track you yet, write to us. Being listed in the price table is free and never paid for.

The rules

  • Every sponsored placement is clearly labelled and uses rel="sponsored" links.
  • Sponsors never change price rankings, sort order or stock data. The cheapest in-stock listing is always shown first.
  • We only take sponsors we would be comfortable sending readers to (real stock, consistent prices, normal payment methods).

Honest numbers

The site launched in October 2026, so we do not have meaningful visitor numbers yet and will not make any up. Early sponsors get introductory pricing, and we'll share real traffic figures as soon as we have them.
