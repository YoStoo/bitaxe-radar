Free price-drop alerts

Get one email when a miner drops to your price. Pick a miner and a target price (USD). We email you only when it is in stock at or under that price. Free; unsubscribe with one click.

Double opt-in: after you submit, we send one confirmation email, and you only start getting alerts once you click the link in it. Alerts go out at most when our checks (every ~6 hours) find a new lower price, and at most about once a week per price. Your email is only used for these alerts and is never sold.
